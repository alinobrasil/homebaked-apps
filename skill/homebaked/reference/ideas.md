# App ideas

Things people can ask for, phrased the way they might say them. Suggest 3–5 that fit the
person; don't recite the list. Every one of these works offline unless marked 🌐.

## Everyday helpers
- "Make me a tip calculator that splits the bill between friends."
- "A shopping list I can tick off in the store, sorted by aisle."
- "A countdown to my holiday, with the days left in big numbers."
- "A water tracker: tap a glass each time I drink, resets at midnight."
- "A chore chart for the kids with stars they earn each day."
- "A habit tracker for three habits, showing my streaks."
- "A kitchen timer with several timers running at once, each with a name."
- "A unit converter for cooking: cups, grams, ounces, spoons."
- "A medicine reminder list showing what I've taken today." (no alarms, just a checklist)
- "A simple budget: what I've spent this week against a limit."

## Learning
- "Flashcards for my Spanish vocabulary, with the phone saying each word out loud."
- "A times-tables game for my 8-year-old that gets harder as they improve."
- "A spelling game for these words: …, where the phone reads the word and my kid types it."
- "A quiz about world capitals with a high score."
- "A flag quiz: show a flag, pick the country from four choices."

## Games
- "A memory matching game with animal emojis for a 4-year-old."
- "Tic-tac-toe for two people on one phone, with a score counter."
- "A word scramble game using words my kid is learning."
- "A dice roller for board game night, with big dice and a shake to roll."
- "Snake that I control by swiping."
- "A reaction-time game to play against a friend on one phone."

## Calm and wellbeing
- "A breathing exercise: a circle that grows and shrinks, 4 seconds in, 6 out."
- "A gratitude journal: three things a day, kept only on my phone."
- "A meditation timer with a soft bell at the start and end."

## Apps that work together
- "Make my spelling game give points, and let the points unlock extra levels in my puzzle
  game." (shared storage between the two apps)
- "A family reward chart where stars earned can be spent in a little game."

## Needs the internet 🌐 (only when the person wants live data)
- "Today's weather for my city, big and simple." (a free weather API, no account)
- "Sunrise and sunset times for where I live this week." (a free API, cached for offline)
